Her Story

Eighteen Years Inside a Construction Company

When Carolyn joined her brother's company it was sheds. Small buildings, lot sales, customers walking on and walking off. There was no master plan, just a small business and a lot of figuring things out in the moment.

Over 18 years that company moved from shed sales into post frame buildings and barndominiums, which changed what they could build and who they could sell to. Carolyn built most of the machinery behind it. She ran the early sales conversations herself, then hired and developed a 12-person sales team, and replaced a pile of manual habits with systems the whole company could follow.

"More than $150 million in construction sales didn't happen by accident. It happened because we built the right process, trained the right people, and created systems that worked every single day — not just when I was watching."

She built the ALS Framework™ (Ask. Listen. Solve.) after watching too many salespeople lose deals they should have won, almost always because they skipped past the conversation. The Streamlined Construction System came out of the same years, watching disconnected software and fuzzy processes quietly drain a company.

She took all of that into CSM Synergy: a construction consulting practice for the one industry she actually knows from the inside.
